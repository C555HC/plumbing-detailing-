# Ошибки и ограничения запуска

Сохранены наблюдения по каждой версии. unknown означает непроверенность, а не доказанную техническую ошибку.

## Попытка 1

Проверка 1:
- impeller-shape / unknown: Precise curved-blade profile cannot be compared to manufacturer; omitted there.
- impeller-placement_connections / unknown: Wheel behind perforatedplate at left hydraulicend, but lower inlet connection to central eye obscured.
- rotor-material / unknown: Exact rotor alloy unconfirmed.
- stator-material / unknown: Copper-looking winding and grey stack, materials not specified in relied-on source.
- shaft-placement_connections / unknown: Common horizontal axis, but longitudinal shaft channel not exposed.
- cooling-path / fail: Plate apertures are visible, but solid-looking shaft has no visible hollow returnbore.
- controls / fail: Terminalbox migrated to far right ofmotor rather than frontleft store position; cable shortened offcrop.
- captions / unknown: Unlabelledbase, captioncheck unknown.
